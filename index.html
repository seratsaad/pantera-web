Hunting the most metal-rich stars in the solar neighborhood
Explore the survey Paper I

56 metal-rich stars  ·  3 spectrographs  ·  25 ultra-metal-rich ([Fe/H] > 0.4)  ·  up to [Fe/H] = +0.58

Galactic chemical-evolution models predict that stars far more iron-rich than the Sun should not form near it — yet they are here. PANTERA finds them, and measures how metal-rich they really are.
