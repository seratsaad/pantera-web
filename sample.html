Sample & Instruments

Three telescopes, one metal-rich tail

We drew targets from ~3,100 metal-rich candidates that pass our metallicity, distance, magnitude, and astrometric-quality cuts, then observed the brightest reachable stars in the 2026 semesters. They span Teff ≈ 4435–6236 K and log g ≈ 2.2–4.5, from cool giants through subgiants to warm dwarfs.

PEPSI
Large Binocular Telescope
31 stars

R ≈ 120,000, 200 µm fiber, ~4800–7400 Å. Median S/N ≈ 300 per resolution element.

Levy
Automated Planet Finder
14 stars

R ≈ 110,000, 3740–9700 Å, Decker-W slit. Median S/N ≈ 140.

HIRES
Keck I Telescope
17 stars

R ≈ 50,000, ~3600–8000 Å, California Planet Search setup. Median S/N ≈ 190.

Six stars were observed with more than one spectrograph, giving 62 spectra of 56 distinct stars and six pairs for cross-instrument checks.
